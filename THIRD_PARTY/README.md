# Third-party mathematical source

The source modules preserve their original authorship and upstream references.
The corresponding licenses, notices and modification records are retained here.

- `GlobalEmbedding.External.SphereEversion`: Sphere Eversion, Apache-2.0.
- `GlobalEmbedding.External.OpenAIMath`: OpenAI math, Apache-2.0.
- `GlobalEmbedding.External.CalabiYau`: Calabi-Yau and its differential-geometry
  and DeGiorgi sources, Apache-2.0; see the nested notices.
- Mathematical material adapted from Poincare Conjecture retains its original
  Apache-2.0 license and notice.

The files were extracted, adapted and namespaced in the original mathematical
development. This repository groups their existing Lean modules into topic
directories while preserving their module names and exact proof source.
